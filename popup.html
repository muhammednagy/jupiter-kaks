How to watch

Open a Jupiter show and turn on Estonian subtitles. English is prepared ahead, even while paused, and appears underneath in yellow. Give it a few seconds to prepare before pressing play.

Free, no API key. Caption text is sent to the University of Tartu’s Neurotõlge service; translations are saved on this device. Preparation resumes automatically after temporary service limits. Machine translations can make mistakes.
